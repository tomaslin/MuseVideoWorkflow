# Department packet template

One file per department, per project, in `projects/<slug>/departments/`.
A packet that says only "costumes: robes" is a placeholder and fails
evaluation. A packet must answer, for its department, in this project:

- What does this department own, shot by shot?
- What are its continuity anchors (objects, fabrics, colours, sounds
  that must not change unless a shot changes them on purpose)?
- What changes, and when, and why (wear stages, damage, light, sound)?
- What are its per-shot notes, keyed by shotId?
- What would break if this department were missing?

If a department has nothing to deliver for a shot, say so explicitly
and say why — "no extras in this shot because the house is empty"
is a decision; a blank field is not.
