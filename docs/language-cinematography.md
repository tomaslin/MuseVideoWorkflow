# Cinematography language for shot definitions

Every shot record uses these fields with these meanings. Pick from the
lists; do not invent a new shot size halfway through a project.

## Shot size (distance from subject)

- **Extreme wide (EWS):** landscape, the person is small in the world.
- **Wide (WS):** full body, place and person together.
- **Full shot (FS):** head to toe, action readable.
- **Medium wide (MWS):** knees up.
- **Medium (MS):** waist up, the workhorse of conversation.
- **Medium close-up (MCU):** chest up.
- **Close-up (CU):** face, or a single object that matters.
- **Extreme close-up (ECU):** eyes, hands, a detail that carries meaning.

## Camera movement

- **Locked off:** no movement. Stillness is a choice, not a default.
- **Push-in (dolly in):** slow approach; increases intimacy or dread.
- **Pull-back (dolly out):** reveals context; often a release.
- **Pan:** horizontal turn on a fixed axis.
- **Tilt:** vertical turn on a fixed axis.
- **Tracking / dolly:** camera moves with or alongside the subject.
- **Crane / rise:** vertical movement, usually a reveal or a farewell.
- **Handheld:** human, unstable, immediate. Use sparingly; if every shot is
  handheld, none of them is.

## Lens, depth, lighting, color

- **Lens:** wide (28mm and below, expansive, distorted edges), normal
  (35–50mm, human), long (85mm and above, compressed, isolating).
- **Depth of field:** shallow isolates a subject; deep keeps the world
  present. State it, or the generator will choose at random.
- **Lighting:** key, fill, practicals, golden hour, overcast, hard noon,
  single-source night. Name the key light and its direction.
- **Color:** palette in 3–5 named colors, plus a rule (for example:
  "warm practicals against cold daylight, saturation pulled back 30%").
  A palette is not "moody" — mood is not a color.
- **Composition:** rule of thirds, leading lines, negative space, foreground
  framing, symmetry vs. asymmetry. One compositional idea per shot.

## A shot record must contain

Size, movement, lens intent, lighting, one action, one emotional beat,
location, characters present, and the locked look/character blocks verbatim.
A shot missing any of these is not ready for generation.
