# Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi — trailer script (original lines only)

All narration and dialogue below are original, written for this
trailer. No lines are quoted from any existing work.

## Narration (audio script only — never in a video prompt)

- s01–s03 (dry, amused voice): "In the desert realms, the great sects
  guard the roads. The beggars guard what's left."
- s04–s06 (voice drops, quieter): "But when the sand gives back its
  dead, no sect comes. Only the ones with nothing to lose."
- s07–s09 (rising, then cut): "They asked for a bowl of rice. They
  got a war. — Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi."

## On-screen action beats (no dialogue in clips)

- s01: Band crosses dunes at dawn, laughing, ragged.
- s02: Chief Hong plants his staff at the ruined caravanserai.
- s03: Little Sparrow watches the storm on the horizon, smile fading.
- s04: Jiangshi hop out of sand tombs at night, in formation.
- s05: Chief Hong strikes the first vampire with his staff.
- s06: A talisman flutters loose from a vampire's forehead.
- s07: The full band fights across the dunes, staffs and ropes.
- s08: Little Sparrow leaps over three vampires at once.
- s09: The band stands silhouetted at dawn; a second tomb door
  grinds open in the far distance. Title sting (added in assembly,
  not generated in a clip).
