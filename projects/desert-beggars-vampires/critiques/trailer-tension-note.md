# Note: trailer tension ending — evaluator finding waived, with reason

The evaluator flags one minor finding: "Final tension above 4 — the
film does not resolve, it stops." For a feature, that would be a
real flaw. For this trailer, it is the design.

This project ends at tension 8, not 2: the band stands silhouetted
at dawn while a second tomb door grinds open in the far distance.
A trailer must leave a hook, not a resolution. Resolving to 2 would
tell the audience the story is over, which is the opposite of a
trailer's job.

Waived by this note, 2026-10-29, for this project only. The waiver
does not apply to feature projects, where the resolution rule stands.
