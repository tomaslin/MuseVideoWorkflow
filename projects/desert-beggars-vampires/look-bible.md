# Look bible: Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi

## Style in one sentence
Wuxia desert epic, painterly and operatic, with dynamic wire-fu
camera — sweeping crane and tracking moves, dust always in the air.

## Palette
- ochre sand
- blood-red dusk
- cold moonlit blue night
- tattered grey-brown beggar robes
- rotting dark Qing robes with faded gold trim
- Rule: warm desert days against cold nights; no modern objects,
  no saturated neon, no clean costumes — everything is worn, patched,
  or rotting.

## Light
Harsh desert sun by day, blood-red low sun at dusk, cold moonlight
at night with warm lantern light at the caravanserai. Night is never
pitch black — moonlight defines the hopping silhouettes.

## Lens and camera
Wide anamorphic feel, sweeping crane and tracking moves for action,
locked-off for the two scare beats (s04, s06) so the hopping reads
clearly. No drone shots that break the wuxia framing, no shaky-cam.

## Wardrobe and world rules
Beggars: patched grey-brown robes, rope belts, bare feet or straw
sandals, staffs, bowls, gourds. Jiangshi: stiff dark Qing robes,
faded gold trim, paper talismans on foreheads, arms held stiffly
forward, green-grey skin. The desert: dunes, ruined caravanserai,
buried tomb doors, sandstorm haze.

## Grain and finish
Fine film grain, high contrast, dust and sand particles visible in
light beams.

## Locked look block (copied verbatim into every prompt)
Wuxia desert epic in painterly live-action style, ochre sand and blood-red dusk against cold moonlit blue night, tattered grey-brown beggar robes against ornate dark Qing robes, dust and sand in the air, dynamic wuxia wire-fu camera with sweeping crane and tracking moves, high contrast, no modern objects, no saturated neon.
