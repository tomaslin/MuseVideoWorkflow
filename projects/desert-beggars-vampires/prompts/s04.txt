Wuxia desert epic in painterly live-action style, ochre sand and blood-red dusk against cold moonlit blue night, tattered grey-brown beggar robes against ornate dark Qing robes, dust and sand in the air, dynamic wuxia wire-fu camera with sweeping crane and tracking moves, high contrast, no modern objects, no saturated neon. hopping vampires in rotting dark Qing robes with faded gold trim, paper talismans on their foreheads, arms held stiffly forward, green-grey skin, moving in stiff, hopping formation Shot: WS, locked-off, sand tombs at night. Action: hop out of the sand tombs in formation Emotional beat: the scare, in full view Sound mood: rhythmic hopping thuds, dry cloth rustle. Background: Foreground: tomb steps half-buried in sand. Midground: three tomb doors with carved lintels, the centre door open and dark. Deep background: dune face at night, moonlit, no vegetation. Camera choreography: Locked-off: camera does not move, so the hopping formation reads clearly against the dune face, wide framing with all tomb doors visible. Choreography: Beat 1 (0-3s): Darkness in the centre tomb door, dust falling. Beat 2 (3-7s): Jiangshi hop out in formation, in unison, one hop per second. Beat 3, payoff (7-10s): The line of vampires holds formation facing the caravanserai lights, hold on the formation. Lighting: Cold moonlight from above, lantern light from the caravanserai far behind the camera, tomb interiors pitch dark. Lens: 35mm, focus: Deep focus: tomb doors, vampires, and dune face all sharp.. Motion: Subject motion: stiff hopping in unison, arms forward. Background motion: dust falling from the tomb door, nothing else. Camera still, subjects move — the horror is in the contrast. Background performers: No beggar extras; the vampires are the only figures, in a line of five. World rules: Jiangshi rules 1, 2, 3, 5: hop only, blind, no speech, no tiring, arms stiffly forward. Rule 4: eyes closed or downcast while sealed.
