# Character sheet: Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi

## Chief Hong — leader of the beggar band
- Role: protagonist, beggar chief
- Locked description (verbatim in every prompt featuring him):
  a weathered one-eyed beggar chief in his 60s with a grey beard, patched grey-brown robes, a rope belt, and a knotted wooden staff, moving with slow, deliberate authority
- Reference image: projects/desert-beggars-vampires/characters/chief-hong-reference.jpg
  (to be generated first on a real run, supplied to every clip)
- Voice traits (audio script only): dry, amused, unhurried.

## Little Sparrow — young beggar fighter
- Role: second lead, the band's fastest fighter
- Locked description (verbatim in every prompt featuring her):
  a lean young beggar woman in her 20s with short black hair tied back, patched grey-brown robes, straw sandals, and a rope whip at her belt, moving fast and light across the sand
- Reference image: projects/desert-beggars-vampires/characters/little-sparrow-reference.jpg
  (to be generated first on a real run, supplied to every clip)

## The Jiangshi — hopping vampires
- Role: antagonists, the risen dead of the tomb-city
- Locked description (verbatim in every prompt featuring them):
  hopping vampires in rotting dark Qing robes with faded gold trim, paper talismans on their foreheads, arms held stiffly forward, green-grey skin, moving in stiff, hopping formation
- Reference image: projects/desert-beggars-vampires/characters/jiangshi-reference.jpg
  (to be generated first on a real run, supplied to every clip)
- No dialogue. Sound: rhythmic hopping thuds, dry cloth rustle.
