# Character sheet: Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi

## Chief Hong — leader of the beggar band
- Role: protagonist, beggar chief
- Locked description (verbatim in every prompt featuring him):
  a weathered one-eyed beggar chief in his 60s with a grey beard, patched grey-brown robes, a rope belt, nine small cloth bags tied across his back marking him sect chief, and a knotted wooden staff, moving with slow, deliberate, fluid authority, weight shifting with each step
- Reference image: projects/desert-beggars-vampires/characters/chief-hong-reference.jpg
  (to be generated first on a real run, supplied to every clip)
- Voice traits (audio script only): dry, amused, unhurried.

## Little Sparrow — young beggar fighter
- Role: second lead, the band's fastest fighter
- Locked description (verbatim in every prompt featuring her):
  a lean young beggar woman in her 20s with short black hair tied back, patched grey-brown robes, straw sandals, five small cloth bags at her belt marking her scout rank, and a rope whip at her belt, moving fast and light across the sand, with fluid weight and a crouch-and-settle landing
- Reference image: projects/desert-beggars-vampires/characters/little-sparrow-reference.jpg
  (to be generated first on a real run, supplied to every clip)
- Voice traits (audio script only): quick, bright, teasing.

## The Jiangshi — hopping vampires
- Role: antagonists, the risen dead of the tomb-city
- Locked description (verbatim in every prompt featuring them):
  hopping vampires in rotting dark Qing robes with faded gold trim, paper talismans on their foreheads, arms held stiffly forward with fingers together, legs perfectly straight with knees locked and never bending, torso rigid and upright, green-grey skin, hopping as a single rigid body from the ankles with no crouch, no knee lift, no walking and no running, moving in stiff, hopping formation
- Reference image: projects/desert-beggars-vampires/characters/jiangshi-reference.jpg
  (to be generated first on a real run, supplied to every clip)
- No dialogue. Sound: rhythmic hopping thuds, dry cloth rustle.
