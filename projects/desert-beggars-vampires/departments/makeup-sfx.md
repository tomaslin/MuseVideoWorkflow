# Makeup and SFX: Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi

## Beggars

- Skin: sun-darkened, dust in the creases of the hands and neck,
  chapped lips. Hong has a scar over his blind left eye — the eye
  itself is milky, never moving to track action. Sparrow has a
  small scar on her right cheekbone, visible in close shots.
- Sweat and dust progression: s01 light dust, s03 wind-blown hair
  and sand on the face, s07–s08 heavy sweat and dust, s09 dawn
  light showing dried sweat lines. Makeup must progress, not reset.

## Jiangshi

- Skin: green-grey pallor, dry and cracked, darkest at the
  knuckles, elbows, and around the eyes. Eyes: in s04, closed or
  downcast; in s06, after the talisman loosens, fully open and
  black. That eye change is the scare beat and must not happen
  earlier.
- Talisman SFX: paper flutter is a practical effect — the
  talisman lifts at one corner in s05 (wind + staff impact),
  flutters in s06, and is gone by s07 for the lead vampire only.
- Tomb doors: the second tomb door in s09 grinds open by 2–3
  centimetres of visible movement, with dust falling from the
  lintel. It must not swing open fully — a hook is a door
  beginning to open, not a door open.
- Dust and sand: practical dust in every exterior shot, at a
  rate that matches the wind (light in s01, heavy in s03's storm
  approach, settling in s09's dawn). Dust that appears in one
  shot and vanishes in the next breaks weather continuity.
