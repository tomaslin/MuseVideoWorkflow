# Production design: Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi

## Locations (three, no more — a trailer must not sprawl)

1. **Open desert dunes** (s01, s07, s08, s09): rolling ochre dunes,
   wind-rippled crests, a single dead tree on a ridge in the far
   distance that must appear in s01 and s09 to rhyme the opening
   and closing. Foreground: loose sand and footprints. Midground:
   the band. Deep background: dunes to the horizon, haze, no
   vegetation, no modern structures.
2. **Ruined caravanserai** (s02, s03, s05, s06): a walled courtyard
   with one broken arch gate, a wellhead, a lantern line strung
   between two posts, and a roofline Sparrow can stand on in s03.
   The arch must be the same arch in every caravanserai shot: same
   break pattern (top-left voussoir missing), same lantern count
   (five, three lit at night). Dressing: broken cart wheel, stacked
   water jars, torn awning cloth that moves in wind in every shot.
3. **Sand tombs** (s04, and the second tomb door in s09): low,
   half-buried stone doors with carved lintels, set into a dune
   face. The first tomb's door is open and dark in s04; the second
   tomb's door, far away in s09, is closed at the start of the shot
   and grinding open by the end. The lintel carving is the same
   motif in both — a sun disc with a line through it — so the
   audience connects them without narration.

## Background layers per shot

- s01: foreground footprints, midground band, deep dunes + dead tree.
- s02: foreground gate threshold, midground courtyard, deep arch + wellhead.
- s03: foreground roof tiles, midground Sparrow, deep storm wall on horizon.
- s04: foreground tomb steps, midground tomb doors, deep dune face at night.
- s05: foreground courtyard dust, midground Hong vs vampire, deep lantern line.
- s06: foreground vampire's shoulder, midground talisman, deep courtyard dark.
- s07: foreground sand spray, midground band vs vampires, deep dunes at night.
- s08: foreground three vampires, midground Sparrow in leap, deep moon.
- s09: foreground band silhouettes, midground dunes, deep second tomb door.

## What must not change

The arch, the lantern count, the dead tree, and the lintel motif
are continuity anchors. If any of them changes between shots, the
world changes with it, and the audience feels it before they can
name it.
