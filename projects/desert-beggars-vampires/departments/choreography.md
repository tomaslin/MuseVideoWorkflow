# Choreography: Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi

Every fight shot is written as numbered beats with timing inside
its 10 seconds. A beat is one clear action. No shot contains more
than three beats, and the last beat is always the payoff.

## s05 — Hong vs the first vampire (MS, tracking)

- Beat 1 (0–3s): Vampire hops forward, arms stiff, toward Hong.
  Hong plants his feet, staff grounded.
- Beat 2 (3–7s): Hong swings the staff in a low arc, striking
  the vampire's stiff arms. The vampire staggers back one hop,
  talisman corner lifting.
- Beat 3, payoff (7–10s): Hong holds the staff across his body,
  breathing hard, vampire reeling but not fallen. Hold on Hong's
  face. Cut on the hold, not mid-swing.

## s07 — Full band battle (WS, crane)

- Beat 1 (0–3s): Band and vampires meet in the dunes. Staffs
  rise, ropes swing. Spatial layout: beggars left and centre,
  vampires right, moon behind the vampires so they read as
  silhouettes.
- Beat 2 (3–7s): Three simultaneous exchanges, each readable:
  Hong staff vs vampire (centre), Sparrow rope vs vampire
  (left), two extras with bowls and short staffs (right).
  No exchange may cross another's space.
- Beat 3, payoff (7–10s): A vampire is knocked back into the
  sand, dust bursting. Band holds formation. Cut on the dust
  burst, not mid-blow.

## s08 — Sparrow's leap (MS, tracking)

- Beat 1 (0–3s): Sparrow runs toward three vampires in a line,
  gathering speed, rope whip trailing.
- Beat 2 (3–7s): She leaps, wire-assisted, in a high arc over
  all three vampires. Camera tracks at her height, not below.
  The three vampires hop in place beneath her, arms up, missing.
- Beat 3, payoff (7–10s): Landing in a crouch in the sand,
  sand spraying forward, Sparrow already turning to face them.
  Hold on the landing crouch. The leap without a landing is
  not a move; it is a missing ending.

## Non-fight movement choreography

- s01: Band walks in a loose line, not a military column.
  Hong leads, Sparrow weaves between the others, laughing.
  Footsteps must match the dune slope — uphill steps are
  shorter, with sand slipping back.
- s04: Jiangshi hop in formation, in unison, at a steady
  rhythm (roughly one hop per second). No vampire breaks
  formation, no vampire runs, no vampire stumbles. Unison is
  the horror.
- s09: Band stands still. Stillness after battle is the beat.
  No one walks, no one celebrates. The only movement is the
  second tomb door, far away.
