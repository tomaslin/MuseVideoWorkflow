# Sound and music: Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi

## Sound design per shot (not a mood — a list)

- s01: wind over sand (low, constant), footsteps in sand,
  distant laughter, no music yet. Music entering at s01 would
  tell the audience how to feel before they have met anyone.
- s02: staff thud in sand (single, dry), cloth in wind, a
  single distant caravan bell, then silence for the last two
  seconds as Hong looks at the horizon.
- s03: wind rising in pitch, sand hissing, Sparrow's breath,
  no music. The storm is the score here.
- s04: rhythmic hopping thuds (one per second, in unison),
  dry cloth rustle, no music, no voices. Unison thuds are the
  scare.
- s05: staff crack (sharp, woody), hopping thud stopping for
  one beat after the strike, Hong's breath. Music: a single
  low drum hit on the strike, then nothing.
- s06: paper flutter (close, dry), then sudden silence for
  three seconds. Silence after the talisman loosens is the
  loudest sound in the trailer.
- s07: battle cries (ragged, not martial), staff cracks,
  rope whistles, wind. Music enters here for the first time:
  driving percussion, no melody yet.
- s08: whoosh on the leap, sand spray on landing, a single
  melodic phrase on a bowed string as Sparrow lands. That
  phrase is the trailer's theme, heard once, in full, here.
- s09: dawn wind, distant stone grinding (low, slow), the
  theme phrase again, quieter, then cut to silence on the
  title. The title sting is silence, not a chord.

## Music rules

- No music in s01–s04. Percussion only in s07. Theme only in
  s08 and s09. A score that never stops is wallpaper; a score
  that enters late means something.
- Dialogue is a separate stem, never in a video prompt. The
  narration lines in script.md are recorded separately and
  mixed under, never baked into a clip.

## Mix targets (for the assembly stage)

- Dialogue/narration intelligible above music at all times.
- SFX (staff, hopping, flutter) above music in their shots.
- Loudness: trailer mix, not feature mix — peaks on strikes
  and the leap, valleys on s02's silence and s06's silence.
