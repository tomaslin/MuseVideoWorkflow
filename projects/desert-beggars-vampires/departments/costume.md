# Costume: Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi

## Beggars — wear as biography

- **Fabric:** coarse hemp and patched wool, grey-brown, never
  clean, never matching. Patches are hand-stitched, irregular,
  in darker and lighter greys; no two beggars share a patch pattern.
- **Chief Hong:** longest robe (ankle length, frayed hem), rope
  belt with three knots, a wine gourd on a cord, straw hat with a
  torn brim. Wear stage: dust-caked at the hem from the dunes in
  s01, sand-dusted throughout, no blood until s07, when a tear
  opens at his left shoulder — that tear must persist in s09.
- **Little Sparrow:** shorter robe (knee length, for running),
  rope whip coiled at her belt, straw sandals with one broken
  strap (right foot, visible in s03 and s08). Her robe is lighter
  than Hong's — she is the fastest, so she wears the least.
- **Band extras:** five to seven beggars in s01 and s07, each
  with a distinct patch pattern and a distinct prop (bowl, rope,
  short staff, sack, flute). No extra may wear a clean garment.
- **After battle (s09):** every beggar's robe is torn at least
  once, dust-caked, and damp with sweat at the collar. No one
  looks fresh at dawn.

## Jiangshi — decay as uniformity

- **Robes:** dark Qing robes, once ornate, now rotting: faded
  gold trim only at the collar and cuffs, tears at the hem and
  elbows, dust and sand ingrained. All vampires share the same
  trim pattern — they are a tomb's court, not individuals.
- **Talismans:** yellow paper, black ink characters (generic
  seal script, no real-world text copied), pasted centre-forehead.
  Condition is a continuity chart: pristine in s04, fluttering in
  s05, loose in s06 for the lead vampire only, missing for that
  vampire from s06 onward. No other vampire loses a talisman.
- **Skin and hands:** green-grey, dry, cracked at the knuckles.
  Hands are held stiffly forward, fingers together, never relaxed.
- **What never happens:** a jiangshi never wears clean robes,
  never removes its own talisman, never changes trim between shots.

## Bag ranks — the hierarchy, on camera (added 2026-10-29)

- Bag count is rank, and it must be legible: 9 bags Chief Hong
  (across the back), 8 bags elder deputy, 7 bags hall masters,
  5 bags Sparrow (at the belt), 3 bags rank-and-file, 1 bag
  newcomers. Bags are small, patched cloth pouches, tied with
  rope, never clean, never matching.
- In s01 the band walks in rank order (Hong, elder, hall
  masters, rank-and-file); in s07 the same order holds in
  battle formation. A shot where Hong's 9 bags are not visible,
  or where an extra appears to outrank him, is a failed shot.
