# Desert Beggars vs the Jiangshi — brief

- Premise: A band of beggars, the lowest sect in the jianghu, is the
  only force standing between a desert caravanserai and a tomb of
  hopping vampires (jiangshi) awakened by a sandstorm. The trailer
  sells the joke and the threat at once: nobody expects beggars to
  save anyone, least of all beggars.
- Mood: Wuxia epic, dusty and operatic — ochre desert days, blood-red
  dusk, cold moonlit nights. Wire-fu action, but grounded in the
  beggars' ragged, practical fighting style: staffs, bowls, ropes,
  and anything to hand.
- Duration: 90 seconds (9 x 10-second shots, 2 batches of 6 and 3).
  This is a trailer, not a feature: it teases, it does not resolve.
  Tension ends high (8), on a hook, by design — see storyboard.
- Audience: Adult viewers who know wuxia and jiangshi folklore.
  Language: English narration lines are original; any Chinese terms
  used are generic folklore terms (jianghu, jiangshi), not quoted
  material from any existing work.
- Emotional arc: scorned outsiders → impossible threat → ragged,
  joyful defiance → dawn after battle, with the question left open:
  what woke the tomb?
- This is an original story. No characters, dialogue, music, or
  footage from any existing film, series, or game are used.
