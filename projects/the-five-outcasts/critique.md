# Development critique — before generation

The old concept had group spectacle without an ethical engine. This version has a false rescue premise, visible evidence contradicting it, and a decision left unresolved. The risk is overcrowding: ten named looks plus two armies can become an unreadable montage. Only the two heroes and Wen carry spoken conflict. The five are introduced once as a countable tableau; ring, bowl and flute isolates carry agency and power without biographies.

The twist must be visible, not just spoken. Reject s11 if Wen has bindings or an extra controlling hand; reject s12 if Ash restrains her; reject s13 if she looks entranced. s01 must show mutually killed red/indigo soldiers before necromancy. Reject the ending if corpses attack the city or the five become saintly smiles. Their handling of dead bodies stays horrific even when their defensive motive becomes clear.

The word Heavenly is social status, not a license for frictionless flights. Action is three readable choices: cut, deflect, stop. Corpse gore is brief, stylized stagecraft. The huge s18 tableau is the likeliest generation failure; if identities collapse, split its coverage into two explicit sources rather than accepting merged bodies. Source handles and trims are provisional until footage review. No video is shipping-ready from this document alone.
