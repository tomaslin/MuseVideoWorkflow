# Costume, makeup and practical gore

Heroes start with immaculate white/ivory silk; a right-cuff blood stain transfers in s08 and persists. Wen's travel robe is worn, repaired and freely belted; wrists never tied. Five outcasts have individually fixed prosthetics: Rook twisted upper back, Reed healed left-arm absence, Bell opaque eyes, Ash fused lower jaw, Heel right club foot. Frame and costume distinguish them without adding horns or demon skin. Use waxen corpse appliances, painted wounds, breakaway rubber forearm and a short thick crimson blood burst. No real footage, medical demonstration or anatomical lingering. Damage belongs principally to the already-dead soldiers; do not substitute violence against the woman for horror. Practical cloth/wax remains material, not melting AI edges.
