# Treatment

The first image is the aftermath of a war nobody won: red and indigo uniforms impaled on each other's weapons, hands still locked together in death. One dead hand tightens. A city voice calls the woman "stolen" before we have seen her. Shen and Lan, the Heavenly Heroes, advance in immaculate silk. Shen raises his cuff against the smell. Their elegance is established as a threat as much as a promise.

Beyond the field, a ruined salt shrine holds five people: Rook, Reed, Bell, Ash and Heel. Their bodies have been injured, altered or born outside the city's idea of perfection. They are not interchangeable monsters or a mass of beggars. Iron, thread, breath, bell and hide form a quiet control system. A turned ring pulls one black thread. Two ranks of dead soldiers stand, red facing indigo, then pivot outward to guard the refuge. The battle predates the outcasts; the dead are their wall, not their victims.

Shen cuts down an approaching corpse. The forearm flies through a short crimson burst. A thread retrieves the severed limb and the corpse stops at the chalk-and-salt boundary rather than pursuing him. Horror is followed by an exact limit. "We were sent for the woman," Lan says. We see Wen's free hand calmly take a drinking bowl. The heroes see captivity; the picture gives the audience agency.

Wen steps between the silks and the five: "I came here myself." Ash lowers his flute to let her speak. A match cut returns to Shen's spotless cuff, now red. "Come back," he orders, never asking. Lan's eyes shift from Wen to the puppet thread wound around Shen's sword: the living heroes are also caught in a story somebody else has written.

The final rush is three simple exchanges, not a crowd simulation: a taut thread deflects the jian; a corpse blocks the gate; Lan stops her blade a hand's breadth from Wen. Rook closes his ring. Both dead squadrons kneel inward, making a shelter around Wen and the five. The heroes stand outside it. "Then who needs rescuing?" Lan asks into the cut. Title: THE FIVE OUTCASTS. A single bell answers from black. No answer to which hero will obey the city.
