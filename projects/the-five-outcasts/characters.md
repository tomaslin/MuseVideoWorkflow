# Cast and locked appearances

## Shen
Shen, an adult male celebrated Heavenly Hero aged thirty, long straight black hair under a narrow silver crown, immaculate white silk swordsman robes with a silver cloud clasp, a straight silver jian in his right hand, clean-shaven angular face, imperious upright posture; his white right cuff stays blood-stained after the first sword strike.

## Lan
Lan, an adult female celebrated Heavenly Hero aged thirty, long black hair in a high crown bun with one silver pin, ivory silk fighting robes and a narrow dark blue sash, a sheathed straight sword at her left hip, composed oval face and an exacting level gaze; her movements are economical and grounded.

## Wen
Wen, an adult city woman aged twenty-eight, black hair in a plain low knot, faded pale-green travel robe with a repaired right sleeve, red city-citizen cord worn loose at her wrist, unbound hands, steady direct gaze; she stands and moves freely and is never chained, gagged or held.

## Rook
Rook, an adult male outcast aged fifty with a pronounced twisted upper back, one high shoulder, a lined watchful face, worn charcoal hemp layers and three heavy iron rings on a cord at his left wrist; he is physically powerful, moves with practiced balance and uses his right hand to turn one ring.

## Reed
Reed, an adult female outcast aged forty, healed left-arm amputation above the elbow, a scar across her left cheek, dark russet patched hemp coat, a bone thread spindle held in her right hand; she routes taut black thread through a wooden belt guide and never grows a replacement arm.

## Bell
Bell, an adult male outcast aged sixty with opaque blind eyes, scarred eyelids, a shaved head, worn smoke-grey hemp robe and a small bronze handbell; he orients his head toward sound, counts footsteps by listening and never tracks an opponent visually.

## Ash
Ash, an adult male outcast aged thirty-five with a visibly healed fused jaw and asymmetrical lower face, loose black hair, faded brown patched coat and a narrow bone flute; he invited Wen to cross the boundary at her request, his damaged jaw does not open normally and he never mouths dialogue.

## Heel
Heel, an adult male outcast aged forty with a pronounced right club foot, broad face, frayed ochre wrap coat and a small hide frame drum at his belt; he plants his adapted right foot before shifting weight onto his sound left leg, never sliding or changing his impairment.

## Red Squadron
The dead Red Squadron: adult soldiers in ruined dark-red lamellar armour, black helmets with short red tassels, blood-blackened cloth and pale corpse faces. They were killed by the Indigo Squadron before this story. Old spear wounds remain visible; black control thread knots at their wrists. Never living bandits, never Qing hopping vampires.

## Indigo Squadron
The dead Indigo Squadron: adult soldiers in broken indigo lamellar armour, black helmets with short blue tassels, blood-blackened cloth and pale corpse faces. They were killed by the Red Squadron before this story. Old sword wounds remain visible; black control thread knots at their wrists. Armour colours never swap.

## Performance and agency
Shen treats revulsion as evidence; his weapon arrives before his question. Lan begins with the same certainty but visibly hesitates when Wen contradicts the commission. Wen speaks plainly and stands by choice beside the people the heroes cannot bear to touch. Ash invited her because she asked. Rook wants solitude and will use an appalling power to keep it. Reed's bowl gesture is practical hospitality rather than sainthood. Bell listens, Heel regulates breath through the drum; no one mugs for a monster reveal.
