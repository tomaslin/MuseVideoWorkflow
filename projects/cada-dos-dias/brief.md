# Brief

A roughly one-minute vertical Spanish-language music video, Cada dos días, in an elegant, old-time 1990s style. Rafa and Teresa meet a verbal ambiguity with curiosity rather than blame, then a professional interpreter helps a health-care team clarify it. The instruction itself is never shown or invented. The song has been recovered from the user’s Muse project; cut timings remain provisional until checked against its beats and lyrics.
