# Creative direction — fiesta revision

A one-minute vertical music video that opens the kitchen door onto a colorful, exuberant Latin American neighborhood street party. The existing Rafa and Teresa story remains a thread among street dancers, singers performing to camera, market life, and an interpreter who helps the conversation move from confusion to clarity. The celebration is the main visual energy; the medical answer is never invented or displayed.

## Look and edit

An imperfect, handmade 1980s television music show: bold patterned clothes, big hair, saturated paint, neighborhood bunting, handheld energy and a little analog tape wear. Play the performances warmly and with humor. Cut largely in 2.5-second segments, interleaving singers and dancing with a few readable story inserts. The closing message remains «Escuchar. Aclarar. Interpretar.» in a clear locally rendered card.

Use generated single-action source clips, inspect faces and hands, and choose short portions. Keep source files and older workprints rather than overwriting them. Singing is an expressive performance: the video generator could not hear the song, so precise lip-sync is not claimed. Short chroma shifts, grain, and tape lines are applied locally after the edit, instead of asking the generator to synthesize a stable glitch effect.

The cut is laid out to a roughly 0.625-second pulse suggested by a basic low-frequency onset analysis of the recovered track. This is an editorial guide, not a claim of a professional beat map, exact lyric sync, or completed sound review. The original song is used as the soundtrack; the optional script dialogue has not been recorded.
