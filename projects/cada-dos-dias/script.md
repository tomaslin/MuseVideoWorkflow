# Script and provisional timing

00–08 La nota: Rafa finds a note, looks at a calendar, and Teresa enters with two cups. Add «Una palabra puede cambiarlo todo». No legible instructions.

08–18 La confusión: Rafa points to consecutive calendar squares; Teresa tilts her head. Transform squares into two question marks in post. Optional Rafa: «¿Dos cada día… o cada dos días?» only over a musical pause.

18–30 Pausa: Teresa raises a hand; freeze briefly. Add separate cards «¿Cuánto?» and «¿Cuándo?» and «No adivines. Pregunta.» Neither card shows a dose.

30–43 Aclarar: a professional interpreter listens to Rafa, asks the health-care team to clarify, listens to their reply, and conveys it. Optional interpreter: «¿Puede confirmar la cantidad y la frecuencia?» The answer is not shown or invented.

43–55 El sentido llega: Rafa nods, relieved. At home Teresa places separate «cantidad» and «frecuencia» cards; they exchange a look and cups gently clink on the beat.

55–60 Cierre: clean background, two speech bubbles joined by a line and «Escuchar. Aclarar. Interpretar.»
