# Treatment

In a sunlit kitchen Rafa turns an unreadable note toward the light and then looks at a calendar. Teresa arrives with two cups. His hesitant fingers mark two neighboring blank squares; her tiny head tilt makes the ambiguity human, not a joke about taking medicine. On-screen Spanish text is placed in post.

Teresa gently raises her hand. A brief freeze gives two questions room to stand apart: ¿Cuánto? and ¿Cuándo? The picture resumes in a fictional consultation, where a professional interpreter listens to Rafa, asks the health-care team to clarify, receives their reply, and relays it. We never see or invent the answer.

Rafa relaxes. At home Teresa sets down the separate ideas cantidad and frecuencia, and the couple share a knowing look. Their cups touch on a real musical accent once the actual track is available. A spare connected-speech-bubble closing card leaves the message: Escuchar. Aclarar. Interpretar.
