# Silkpunk short reel — detailed scene-specific storyboard

You were right: the first version condensed the story into 6 loose clips,
and the combat into one vague 'three at once' clip. That is not what you asked for.
This storyboard does the opposite: it breaks the story into 23 beats, each with
its own position, action, and camera, so a beat can be shot, checked, and cut
without guessing what was supposed to happen in it.

## Pacing — how 23 beats make a 60-second reel

Each beat is generated as a 10-second source clip (the generator's unit),
then cut to its edit length below. 22 beats at 2.5 seconds + the final
quiet beat at 5 seconds = 60 seconds total. The combat is not one beat:
it is 10 beats (D01–D10), each with one attack, one defence, and one recoil,
so positioning and disarms are readable instead of blurred together.

Cast, locked (verbatim in every prompt packet):
- Mad Man: small crippled man, twisted leg, wild crazed grin, unkempt matted hair,
  tattered dust-covered robes, mad-monk energy, alien biomechanical arms from his back.
- Eldest Dame: crimson silk armour, long black hair, spear.
- Middle Dame: jade silk armour, twin-loop hair, twin short blades.
- Youngest Dame: pale ivory-pink silk armour, ribbon sword, wide-eyed and brave.
- Silk Guards: a mix — some are robots (metal/porcelain bodies in pale silk robes, glowing cyber eyes, silk-thread circuits), others are simple humans (ordinary men in pale silk uniforms, no robot parts, no cyber enhancements). Setting for the colony beats: a beautiful Chinese lotus market, with lotus ponds, baskets, stalls, silk banners and lotus lanterns.

Scene sheets (required by the new sheet gate, images still to be approved):
- silk-colony: beautiful silk colony, flowing banners, neon silk threads.
- bamboo-forest: cybernetic bamboo forest, glowing stalks, silk banners.

## Fight style — Jin Yong, all over, not Star Wars

Fights happen over water and inside shops, not on a clean duelling ground.
Choreography is really busy and kinetic: rapid chains, qinggong over the lotus pond,
smashing through silk shops, water spray and silk shreds — overwhelming brutality
in the impacts and wreckage, with small beautiful intricacies inside it: a needle
flicked between fingers, a sword tip tracing a circle, nunchuks spinning back to guard.
Weapons in the fight: hidden needles, double nunchuks, and heavenly sword variations
with laser edges. The lasers are energy on wuxia weapons, not lightsaber duels —
the fighting language is Jin Yong technique, flying and intricate, not Star Wars.
Note: the Dames' sheet images still show their earlier weapons (spear, twin blades,
ribbon sword). Their faces and armour are locked from those sheets; the fight prompts
now use the new weapon set above. If you want the sheet images redone with the new
weapons, say so — otherwise the sheets stay as face/armour references.

## Relic weapons — go bigger, end of universe

Updated direction: the fight weapons are now absolute relic type weapons, end-of-universe scale.
Not ordinary wuxia weapons with a laser edge — ancient relics from the end of the universe:
an absolute relic heavenly sword, massive and old, glowing with ancient energy; absolute relic
double nunchuks forged from end-of-universe relic metal; absolute relic needles, star-forged,
that pierce and stick like relics. They hit bigger — sword strokes split pond water, nunchuks smash
shop beams, needles scatter like relic rain — but the fighting language stays Jin Yong, busy and
kinetic, not Star Wars. Brutality is bigger; the small beautiful intricacies stay inside it.

## Physical sense — and women playing mahjong on water

New rule, applied to every beat: things must make sense physically.
- Water is not a floor. Fighters stand on large sturdy lotus leaves or stone
  stepping stones, never on open water. Leaves dip and spring back under weight.
- Mahjong on water is physically grounded: women play mahjong at a floating
  mahjong table on a stable wooden platform on the lotus pond — four women seated
  on chairs around a solid table, tiles on the table, platform steady, water lapping
  at its edges, not flooding it. Tiles do not float in the water, women do not stand
  in the pond to play.
- Dropped weapons sink and spread rings. Splashed water falls back under gravity.
  Shop beams, counters, and floors hold weight. The ship moves on wheels and hull,
  and the explosion throws debris outward from the wreck, with the Mad Man launched
  by his tendrils pushing off the wreck — not floating unsupported.
These women are background performers in the market/water beats, in addition to the
main cast — they do not change the fight cast count (D05 is still exactly three Dames
plus the Mad Man).
