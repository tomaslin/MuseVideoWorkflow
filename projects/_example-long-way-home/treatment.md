# The Long Way Home — treatment

Opening image: a man walks up a long dirt drive at golden hour, carrying
nothing but a worn jacket over his arm. The house is empty, but not ruined.

Turn: in the workshop he finds a half-finished chair and a letter he never
sent. The objects do not accuse him; they wait. He repairs the chair
instead of throwing it away — the repair is the decision.

Closing image: he leaves at dusk with the porch light on, the chair
standing finished in the window. He does not look back, but he does not
hurry either. The house is no longer a weight; it is a place that was
kept.
