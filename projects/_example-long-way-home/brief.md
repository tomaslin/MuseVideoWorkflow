# The Long Way Home — brief

- Premise: A weathered man in his late 60s returns to the empty house he
  built, and must decide what to keep, what to repair, and what to let go.
- Mood: Desaturated, late-light, quiet — in the mood of a spare acoustic
  farewell, not a copy of any song, lyric, or recording.
- Duration: 240 seconds (24 x 10-second shots, 4 batches of 6).
- Audience: Adult viewers. Language: English, with silence doing as much
  work as words.
- Emotional arc: arrival and avoidance, reckoning with objects that hold
  memory, release — leaving the house lit, not locked in darkness.
- This is an original story. No lyrics, audio, or footage from any
  existing song are used.
