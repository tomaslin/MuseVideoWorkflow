# Look bible: The Long Way Home

## Style in one sentence
Desaturated, late-light, locked-off realism with slow push-ins only,
handheld never.

## Palette
- rust
- faded denim blue
- worn wood brown
- grey sky
- warm lamp amber
- Rule: warm practical lamps against cold blue-grey daylight, saturation
  pulled back, no saturated color anywhere.

## Light
Low golden-hour sun through windows, then warm practical lamps at dusk.
Night is lamp-lit, never pitch black.

## Lens and camera
35mm normal-lens feel, locked-off camera, slow push-ins only. No drone
shots, no fast cuts within a shot.

## Wardrobe and world rules
The man wears the same worn brown work jacket, faded denim shirt, dark
trousers, and scuffed boots throughout. The house is empty but kept:
dust, not ruin. Recurring objects: the chair, the letter, the porch light.

## Grain and finish
Soft natural grain, slightly soft highlights, clean shadows.

## Locked look block (copied verbatim into every prompt)
Desaturated late-light realism, warm practical lamps against cold
blue-grey daylight, muted earth palette of rust, faded denim, worn wood
and grey sky, locked-off camera with slow push-ins only, 35mm normal
lens feel, soft natural grain, no drone shots, no saturated color.
