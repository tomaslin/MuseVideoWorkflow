# Costume: The Long Way Home

- **The man, throughout:** worn brown work jacket over a faded
  denim shirt, dark trousers, scuffed boots. No change of
  clothes across sequences, no hat, no new garment. The jacket
  is the costume: its wear, its frayed cuffs, and its fit
  (slightly loose, as if for a younger man) must be the same
  in s01 and s24.
- **Wear progression:** dust on the boots in s01 (drive),
  sawdust on the jacket and trousers from s11 onward (workshop),
  sawdust brushed off but still present in s17–s18 (sanding
  and finishing), cleanest — but still worn — in s20–s24
  (garden and porch). Dust and sawdust progress with the work;
  they do not reset between shots.
- **What never happens:** a clean jacket, a different jacket,
  a tie, a suit, or any garment that would make the man look
  like a visitor to his own house. He is not a visitor; he
  built the house, and his clothes say so.
