# The Long Way Home — script (beat outline)

This is a visual script: 24 beats, one per 10-second shot, in 3 sequences.
Dialogue is minimal and lives in the audio script, not in video prompts.
Sound moods are per shot in the sequence files.

Sequence 1 — Arrival (s01–s08): he walks the drive, opens the gate,
crosses the porch, enters the hallway, stands in the kitchen, looks at a
photograph on the wall, watches light move across an empty room, and
finally sits — not collapsed, just sitting with it.

Sequence 2 — Reckoning (s09–s16): the workshop, the half-finished chair,
his hands on the wood, the unsent letter read and folded, not torn;
a phone that does not ring, rain on the window, a mirror look that is
honest rather than dramatic, and the first repair: glue, clamp, patience.

Sequence 3 — Release (s17–s24): sanding in morning light, the chair
finished, the garden gate opened, a neighbour's wave returned, the porch
swept, the light switched on, the chair placed in the window, and the
walk back down the drive at dusk — slower, lighter, the house lit
behind him.
