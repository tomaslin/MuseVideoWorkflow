# Chapter 11: Edit, Assembly & Picture Lock

**Billable unit:** Billable unit: one cut (assembly, rough, fine, locked)

**Tension function:** Tension: editing IS tension — shot length, order, and cuts create it.

Inputs: locked clips, audio stems. Outputs: assembly, rough cut, fine cut, picture-locked cut, EDL, change log. Gates: picture lock is a formal state; after lock, story changes require a change request with cost/schedule impact (see docs/change-control.md). Cost drivers: re-edits after lock, re-conforms, re-mixes. Storyboard mapping: the EDL is the storyboard in final order; any shot out of storyboard order must have a recorded reason.
