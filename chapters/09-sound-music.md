# Chapter 09: Sound Script, Score & Mix Plan

**Billable unit:** Billable unit: one sound script + mix plan

**Tension function:** Tension: sound creates tension before picture does — silence, a held note, a sound that stops.

Inputs: script, storyboard. Outputs: sound script (dialogue, SFX list per shot, score cues, silence cues), music mood briefs (never a specific copyrighted recording), mix plan (stems, ducking, loudness target), M&E stem plan for dubbing. Gates: spoken words above music; one or two precise SFX per shot; silence cues are explicit, not accidental gaps. Cost drivers: score cues, Foley/SFX generations, mix time. Storyboard mapping: every panel has a sound cue field; a panel with no sound is a silent film choice, recorded as such.
