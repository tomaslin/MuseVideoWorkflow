# Chapter 13: Quality Control: Technical, Creative, Legal, Accessibility

**Billable unit:** Billable unit: one QC report per master

**Tension function:** Tension: QC protects tension — a sync error or a wrong caption breaks it instantly.

Inputs: finished master. Outputs: QC report (technical: sync, levels, missing frames, aspect, safe zones; creative: arc intact, no placeholder; legal: clearances match ledger; accessibility: captions, AD). Gates: no blocker findings open at shipping; waivers recorded with reason. Cost drivers: QC time, fix-and-recheck cycles. See pipeline/movie.py qc. Storyboard mapping: QC checks panels against clips — intended vs. made, shot by shot.
