# Chapter 07: Casting, Voices & Performance Direction

**Billable unit:** Billable unit: one cast + voice direction pack

**Tension function:** Tension: carried by performance — a flat read flattens a tense scene.

Inputs: script, character bible. Outputs: casting per character, voice traits, performance direction per scene (pace, pause, emotion), recording packets, ADR list. Gates: one voice stem per speaker where possible; voice traits do not assume ethnicity from language; unconfirmed line languages are flagged. Cost drivers: voice generations/recordings per line, ADR redos. Storyboard mapping: a panel's caption must say how the line is said, not just what is said.
