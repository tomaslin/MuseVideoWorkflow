# Chapter 03: Story, Structure & Tension Arc

**Billable unit:** Billable unit: one tension-mapped outline

**Tension function:** Tension: the arc itself — see the curve below.

Inputs: bible. Outputs: premise, character arcs, subplot map, sequence outline with a tension value (0-10) per sequence, turning points, climax, resolution. Gates: every sequence has a dramatic question; every character has a want and a cost; the climax is earned by earlier beats, not announced. Cost drivers: outline revisions are cheap; script revisions after generation are expensive — this is the cheapest place to fix a story. Storyboard mapping: the tension curve is the storyboard in one line. A flat curve means a flat film, no matter how pretty the panels are. Tension targets: setup 2-3, inciting 4, rising 5-7, midpoint reversal 7-8, climax 9-10, resolution 2-3.
