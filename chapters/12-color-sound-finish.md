# Chapter 12: Color Finish, Sound Mix & Titles

**Billable unit:** Billable unit: one finished master (picture + sound + titles)

**Tension function:** Tension: finish must not flatten it — grade and mix serve the arc, not just polish.

Inputs: picture-locked cut. Outputs: graded master, mixed stems (dialogue, music, SFX, M&E), titles/credits, captions (SDH), audio description script. Gates: grade matches color script, mix meets loudness target, captions are faithful and synced, credits are legally complete. Cost drivers: grading session, mix session, captioning, AD. Storyboard mapping: title cards and credit sequences are panels too, with their own timing.
