# Chapter 14: Localization, Distribution & Marketing

**Billable unit:** Billable unit: one deliverables package per platform/language

**Tension function:** Tension: the film must survive translation — meaning, not just words, must carry.

Inputs: finished master, QC report. Outputs: subtitle/dub packages, platform encodes, artwork/poster, trailer/teaser cuts, metadata, delivery spec sheet. Gates: meaning preserved in every language (negation, numbers, uncertainty checked per language), deliverables matrix complete per platform. Cost drivers: per-language dub/sub, per-platform encodes, artwork. Storyboard mapping: trailer is a re-cut storyboard — a new tension curve, built from the same panels, for a different job.
